Help Centre

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about loans, eligibility and investments.

How does Phoenix Financial Services help me get a loan?

We assess your eligibility, compare offers from our partner banks and NBFCs, help you with documentation and follow up with the lender until sanction and disbursement.

Does Phoenix Financial Services lend money directly?

No. We do not lend money directly. Loan approval, interest rates, sanction and disbursement are handled solely by the respective partner bank or NBFC.

What types of loans are available?

Personal Loans, Business Loans, Home Loans, Loan Against Property (LAP), Mortgage Loans, Pension Loans, Working Capital Loans, OD/CC Limits and Balance Transfer Loans.

What documents are generally required?

Usually: identity and address proof (Aadhaar, PAN, passport or voter ID), income proof (salary slips and Form 16 for salaried; ITR and financial statements for self-employed) and bank statements for the last 6–12 months. Property papers or business registration documents are needed for secured or business loans.

How is loan eligibility determined?

Lenders look at your income, existing EMIs, credit (CIBIL) score, job or business stability, business vintage and, for secured loans, the value of the property.

How long does loan processing take?

It depends on the loan type and the lender. Personal loans can be sanctioned within a few days of complete documentation, while home loans and LAP usually take longer because of legal and technical property checks.

Can self-employed people and businesses apply?

Yes. Self-employed professionals, proprietors, partnerships, LLPs and private limited companies can apply for personal, business, working capital and OD/CC facilities, subject to the lender's criteria.

Do you offer loans for pensioners?

Yes. Pension loans are available for eligible pensioners, subject to the lender's age and pension-credit criteria.

What is a Balance Transfer?

A balance transfer moves your existing loan to another lender offering a lower interest rate, which can reduce your EMI or total interest. Some lenders also offer a top-up amount.

What is an OD/CC facility?

An Overdraft (OD) or Cash Credit (CC) limit is a revolving credit line. You can withdraw up to the sanctioned limit as needed, and interest is charged only on the amount you use.

What is a SIP?

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) lets you invest a fixed amount in a mutual fund every month, building wealth steadily through rupee cost averaging and compounding.

Are loan approvals or mutual fund returns guaranteed?

No. Loan approval is always the lender's decision. Mutual fund returns are market-linked and not guaranteed; please read all scheme-related documents carefully.
